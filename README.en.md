 # Target Clients
**Cross-border E-commerce Operations**<br />Commercial activities involving online transactions across different countries or regions. This model leverages internet technology and global logistics networks to break traditional trade barriers, offering consumers more diverse, convenient, and high-quality shopping experiences while providing sellers with broader markets and increased sales opportunities.



 **Overseas E-commerce Operations**<br />Clients engaged in e-commerce abroad manage online sales and transactions on foreign networks, also known as international e-commerce operators. They require systems supporting local languages, integration with regional payment gateways and logistics/warehousing platforms, and the establishment of e-commerce platforms tailored for overseas or local expansion.



 **Mobile E-commerce Operators**<br />These managers or sellers may lack fixed storefronts, office spaces, or maintain long-term mobile work arrangements. They manage product information and orders via the internet and mobile devices. Sellers utilize online store management tools provided by e-commerce platforms or third-party software to handle daily operations like inventory management, order processing, and shipping. Compared to traditional brick-and-mortar stores, this e-commerce model offers greater flexibility and convenience.



 **Requires technologically advanced, stable, and robust systems with secondary development capabilities**<br />Clients seeking cutting-edge technology, highly scalable systems with operational stability, flexible business expansion capabilities, and sufficient development capacity. These clients typically adopt new technologies to enhance operational efficiency and development capabilities while prioritizing system scalability and stability. This ensures better alignment with business needs and future growth, ultimately boosting market competitiveness.



 # Use Cases
BizSpring e-commerce platform achieves full-scenario, omnichannel consumer coverage. Mobile touchscreen stores, tablets, Apple App Store, Android App Store—all unified data operations enable you to open one store and cover all channels.



 **Directly Operated Business Model**<br />Enterprises establish self-operated mall platforms to integrate their channel operations. Through unified management of members, products, orders, finances, and inventory, they centralize oversight of both offline and online multi-channel businesses.



 **Joint Operation Business Model**<br />Integrate multiple channel suppliers through inventory consolidation, unified warehousing, and centralized logistics. The platform incorporates partner suppliers into its self-operated system via consignment or direct sales, establishing multiple self-operated stores to diversify product and service categories while consolidating industry resources. Financial settlements with partner suppliers occur periodically through various methods.



 **Business Recruitment Model**<br />Platform enterprises establish online marketplaces by contracting multi-channel suppliers. They operate the platform through membership, transaction, platform, and financial management. Multi-channel suppliers join the B2B2C platform via franchise agreements.Merchants independently manage product listings, orders, warehousing, logistics, after-sales service, and marketing promotions, settling accounts with the platform periodically. Leveraging the platform's traffic, resources, and marketing services, merchants enhance brand visibility and boost transaction conversion rates. The platform diversifies its product and service offerings, consolidates industry resources, and collects merchant tier fees, category usage fees, and platform commissions.



 **Hybrid Model**<br />Typically combines self-operated + merchant recruitment, self-operated + joint venture + merchant recruitment, etc. Platform companies establish self-operated online stores to integrate their multi-channel operations. Through unified management systems for members, products, orders, finance, and inventory, they coordinate offline and online channels. Simultaneously, they integrate multi-channel suppliers who join the B2B2C platform through franchise agreements.Merchants can independently manage products, orders, warehousing, logistics, after-sales service, and marketing promotions, settling accounts with the platform periodically. Without building their own e-commerce platforms, merchants leverage the large platform's traffic, resources, and marketing services to enhance brand awareness and boost transaction conversion rates. The platform diversifies its product and service offerings, consolidates industry resources, and collects merchant marketing fees, category usage fees, and platform commissions.

**All-Scenario Management Platform**<br />Built with responsive web development, the BizSpring management platform adapts seamlessly to computers, tablets, and mobile devices—accessible anywhere with an internet connection.
